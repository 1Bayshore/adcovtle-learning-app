The Dragon of the Mountain

On a long ago day, King Ivikto XXI of Doikagroi (Dokagrolis) looked out over the city from the balcony of the palace. Low clouds hung over the smoking remains of the Mages Guild, and the surrounding buildings. King Ivikto XXI looked down at the remains of the building, looked up at the sky, and spoke in an angry but firm voice to the people below.

"I swear, by the name of the crown of Doikagroi, that I will have the dragon who caused this slain! I will send our finest warriors and magicians to the source of the Atlent river, where the dragon lives, and we will not stop until the dragon is no longer a threat."

A cheer rang out from the crowd below. King Ivikto XXI watched for a moment, before stepping back into the palace.

Ten days later, two men named Mēo and Kuixa set out on a riverboat. Mēo wore a robe dyed in the finest Doikagroi reds and greens, while Kuixa wore a light suit of armour. As their entourage rowed them up the river, they took turns glaring at each other. Eventually Mēo spoke.

"We should make a plan for how we intend to slay the dragon," he said.

"It is impossible to plan such a task," Kuixa said. "We should arrive, and show our bravery and defend the honour of our kingdom."

"What good is my magic," Mēo asked, "if we do not know when to use it?"

"I suggest you follow my lead then," Kuixa said. "Simply blast the fire the moment before I charge towards the dragon."

"That is most preposterous," Mēo said. "Planning and preparation change the course of the war."

They continued to bicker for the next three days, before they reached first falls. Their riverboat proved too big to portage, so they took a smaller boat, a raft almost, and trudged through the thick forest of tall trees and endless vines. Mēo burnt some of the vines along the way to make room for them to pass.

"This just goes to show," Kuixa said. "You cannot plan for everything. How could you know that there might be such thick vines here?"

"I consulted many a volume about the mysterious lands of Atlent," Mēo said. "I learnt that vines are quite common here."

"How are they 'mysterious' if you know what's there?" Kuixa retorted.

They continued upriver, portaging falls after falls with their smaller crew. The trees grew smaller with each falls, until they climbed the seventh falls and found a barren and rocky landscape with few trees, and even few shrubs. The sun shone brightly down. They left the boat behind and climbed up across the rocks.

"I am glad we prepared for such harsh and unforgiving terrain," Mēo said.

"Your preparations were unnecessary," Kuixa said. "Most of them remain at the bottom of the first falls in the riverboat."

"That is most-" began Mēo, before suddenly an enormous white-scaled dragon appeared over the horizon.

Mēo trained his hands on the enormous dragon as it approached, while Kuixa drew his sword. They watched as the dragon landed on the rocks ahead of them and stared at them through its slit pupils.

"We have come," Kuixa announced, "to bring justice for your destruction of the Mages Guild of Doikagroi. We defend our honour and-"

Suddenly Mēo, who had moved slightly to the side, sent a burst of fire towards the dragon. The dragon howled in rage and spun around towards him.

Kuixa charged at the dragon, attempting to slice it with his sword. But he found that the dragon's hide was thick, and he could only dent its scales. He marched up the dragon's tail onto its back and attempted to cut its massive wings. He left a small scar, but the dragon still flapped its wings in anger.

Mēo climbed atop the dragon, following after Kuixa, and blasted it with fire once more. The dragon spun around once more, requiring the two men on its back to desperately cling on. The dragon then beat its wings and took flight.

Mēo and Kuixa looked on, horrified, as the dragon flew up into the air. It continued back northeast far above the river, and they counted the seven falls as they clang tighter to the dragon's back. They began to tremble as the dragon approached Doikagroi.

As the dragon flew lower over the city, Mēo made a brief prayer to Ifūai, the god of fire. Kuixa reached with one hand and stabbed the dragon with all his might.

The dragon approached the palace, and opened its mouth as they grew close. Then it let out a massive breath of cold air, freezing the palace and causing the stones to crumble. As the palace began to fall to pieces, Kuixa lept from the dragon towards the collapsing palace, falling to his death.

Mēo blasted the dragon with fire one last time, before slipping and falling himself onto the palace. From the courtyard below, King Ivikto XXI looked up at the dragon.

"I swear, by the name of the crown of Doikagroi, that both of the dragons which attacked our city will be slain! I will send our finest remaining warriors to the depths of the caverns below Isudaitēo (Sudasichas), where the dragons live, and we will not stop until the dragon is no longer a threat."

Nobody cheered.
