From the magestic Wiþi mountains to the rolling Cientla mountains, from the ships docked at Paltlon to the Tower of Knowledge in Glisng, Covtle is full of variety. The artists of Mangátle have different lives from the farmers of Alsurea. However, these people have a lanugage in common. This language is Adcovtle.

This book is designed to give you an introduction to Adcovtle, the language of Covtle. By the end of this book, you should be able to maintain a simple conversation in Adcovtle, read signage and make yourself understood. You should also gain an understanding of the culture of Covtle and the lives of the people there. Finally, you will be prepared for the next book in the series, "Adcovtle Beyond the Basics".
